Herald

Herald launch week

Help people understand Herald, a way for DAOs to pay their ambassadors and creators fairly. Post about it, submit the link, and get paid from the DAO's treasury, automatically once you've earned it.

This is a pilot on NEAR testnet. Payouts are in test NEAR, which has no money value. Your posts and feedback are real, and they help shape the product.

How you get paid

Your first two posts

A reviewer checks the link and approves it. You're paid the moment they do.

After two paid posts

You're Trusted. Rate-card posts pay automatically after a 3-minute public window.

Anything unusual

Pick "Something else" with your own amount. A reviewer decides, or the DAO votes on big asks.

Get started in five minutes

  1. Open Meteor Wallet, click MAIN at the top right and switch to Testnet, then create a wallet. Keep your recovery phrase to yourself. Nobody from Herald will ever ask for it.
  2. Your new wallet starts empty. Get free test NEAR at near-faucet.io: paste your wallet address and ask for 1 NEAR. Each submission holds about 0.05 NEAR for storage, and whatever isn't used comes back.
  3. Post your thread, video or Space, and copy its public link.
  4. Open Herald, click Connect wallet, choose Meteor, then open Herald launch week and submit the link.
  5. Watch it on your My record page: every payout and point is public.

House rules

  • Do write in your own voice, tag @NEARProtocol and use #NEARHackathon.
  • Do keep your post up for the whole pilot.
  • Don't buy likes or views, copy someone else's post, or delete a post after it's paid. Any of these gets a public veto, and fraud gets a ban.
  • Don't submit the same post twice.

Dates

The pilot is open now and runs until 11 November 2026. Submissions after that may not be reviewed.

Herald launch week is run by the Herald Pilot DAO on NEAR testnet. Its council is Ade, who leads the project, plus two operator accounts the Herald team runs for the pilot (oliver-herald and amelia-herald). Every payout, veto and vote is public on chain.
