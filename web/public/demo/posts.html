Demo posts

These posts were written by the Herald team for a demo test on NEAR testnet. They are not real X posts, and the demo tester accounts that submitted them (Aminx, Lamz and Zhod) are run by the Herald team. Payouts were made in test NEAR, which has no money value.

Lamzlamz-demo.testnetX thread · demo
  1. Most DAOs pay ambassadors the slow way: a spreadsheet, a monthly vote, and weeks of waiting. Herald fixes that on NEAR. 🧵
  2. The DAO votes once to fund a campaign with a rate card: a thread pays this, a video pays that. The money sits in a contract with no admin key.
  3. New creators get one reviewer. Once you've been paid a few times you're Trusted, and rate-card posts pay you automatically after a short public window.
  4. Big or unusual asks still go to a real DAO vote. Every payout, veto and point is public. #NEARHackathon @NEARProtocol

  3. The clever part: trust is earned per DAO. Your record here never unlocks automatic payouts somewhere else.
